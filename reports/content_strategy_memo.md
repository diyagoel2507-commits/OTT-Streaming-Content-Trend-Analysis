# OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis
## Content Strategy Memo

**Prepared by:** Diya Goel  
**Project:** OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis  
**Platform Analyzed:** Netflix  
**Dataset:** Netflix Movies and TV Shows  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Data Visualization, SQL

---

### 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes Netflix's content catalogue to understand the distribution of movies and TV shows, release-year trends, country-wise content availability, genre diversity, content ratings, and movie duration.

The dataset contains 8,807 titles and 12 columns. Python was used for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and creating six visualizations. SQL queries were also prepared to support category-wise analysis and content aggregation.

The purpose of this analysis is to identify patterns in the catalogue and recommend possible content acquisition and planning strategies.

### 2. Key Findings

**Finding 1: Movies Dominate the Catalogue**

The dataset contains 6,131 movies and 2,676 TV shows. Movies account for approximately 69.6% of titles, while TV shows represent approximately 30.4%.

**Finding 2: Content Is International**

The United States and India are among the leading countries represented in the catalogue. Country-wise analysis can help identify opportunities for regional content planning and greater geographical diversity.

**Finding 3: Genre and Duration Patterns Can Guide Planning**

The genre and movie-duration visualizations help identify the types of content represented most frequently and the duration ranges commonly found in the catalogue. These patterns can inform future content planning, subject to audience-demand validation.

### 3. Actionable Recommendations

**Recommendation 1: Maintain a Balanced Content Portfolio**

Review the current movie-heavy catalogue and evaluate whether additional TV shows, limited series, or other formats could fill audience-demand gaps. Decisions should be based on viewing and engagement data rather than catalogue counts alone.

**Recommendation 2: Strengthen Regional Content Research**

Use country-wise and genre-wise analysis to identify regions and content categories worth investigating further. Validate these opportunities using regional audience preferences, licensing costs, language requirements, and local market research before acquiring content.

**Recommendation 3: Use Genre and Duration Insights for Content Planning**

Compare genre distribution and movie-duration patterns with audience engagement, completion rates, and production or licensing costs. Prioritize content formats that demonstrate strong audience demand and sustainable business value.

### 4. Business Value

This analysis provides a structured overview of Netflix's catalogue and demonstrates how exploratory data analysis and SQL can support content planning. The visualizations make catalogue patterns easier to interpret, while the recommendations translate those patterns into potential business actions.

### 5. Limitations

- The dataset represents a historical catalogue snapshot and does not describe Netflix's current catalogue.
- It does not contain actual viewing hours, completion rates, subscriber behaviour, revenue, or licensing costs.
- Catalogue frequency does not necessarily indicate audience popularity or profitability.
- Some country and genre fields contain multiple comma-separated values, which must be separated carefully for accurate category counts.
- The recommendations are exploratory and should be validated using current audience and business data.

### 6. Conclusion

The project demonstrates a complete introductory analytics workflow covering data inspection, cleaning, visualization, SQL query preparation, and business recommendations. It provides a foundation for more advanced OTT analytics using current catalogue information and audience-performance metrics.
